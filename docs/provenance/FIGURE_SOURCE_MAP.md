# Figure source and study-design map

| Manuscript item | Figure file | Evidentiary role | Required interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main Fig. 1 | `figures/Fig1_update_rules.pdf` | Conceptual update rules and controls | Algorithmic state only; no hardware-efficiency claim |
| Main Fig. 2 | `Fig2b_LDPC_BER_N192_N288.pdf`, `Fig2c_LDPC_FER_N192_N288.pdf` | Independently optimized representative-code benchmark | One representative matrix per size; 10,000 trials/SNR/mode; not a matched-parameter comparison |
| Main Fig. 3 | `Fig3_matched_causal_controls.pdf` | Matched causal controls | Fixed additive-optimized nonmemory parameters; rule-only interventions; paired seed batches |
| Main Fig. 4 | `Fig4a_channel_acquisition.pdf`, `Fig4b_acquisition_retention_summary.pdf` | Acquisition and post-acquisition stability | Default-start acquisition and the correct-start retention intervention remain separate; `make_fig4_acquisition_retention.py` regenerates the lower four-panel summary and renders nonreaching FPT values as `NR` |
| Main Fig. 5 | `Fig5_cross_code_three_way_BER.pdf` | Three-way cross-code fixed transfer | Additive and pSA-specific packages transferred intact; matched lambda=0 is a separate causal ablation; no per-code retuning |
| Supp. Fig. S1 | `Fig2a_LDPC_BER_N96.pdf` | Independently optimized N=96 benchmark | Same design as main Fig. 2 |
| Supp. trajectory diagnostics | `FigS_response_alignment_summary.pdf`, `FigS_response_dynamics_by_size.pdf` | Response alignment and trajectory dynamics | 200 trajectories/cell; shuffled delayed-response distribution preserved |
| Supp. acquisition--stability | `FigS_acquisition_first_passage.pdf`, `FigS_post_acquisition_stability.pdf` | First passage and conditional stability | Nonreaching trajectories remain censored; correct-start results are not natural decoding success rates |
| Supp. initialization robustness | `FigS_initialization_acquisition.pdf`, `FigS_initialization_first_passage.pdf` | Acquisition under all-zero, random, and channel-hard starts | Fixed matched-control parameters; shared initial states and channel realizations; unbounded seed-batch t intervals documented separately |
| Supp. cross-code transfer | `FigS_cross_code_size_summary.pdf`, `FigS_cross_code_mechanism.pdf` | Between-code outcomes and nine-code trajectory sample | Robustness to code realization; not finite-size scaling |
| Supp. MAX-CUT | `FigS_MAXCUT_quality.pdf`, `FigS_MAXCUT_memory_sweep.pdf` | Cross-problem boundary | Small co-optimized differences; selected endpoints may have zero response memory |
| Supp. initial 2-SAT study | `FigS_2SAT_initial_mode_distribution.pdf`, `FigS_2SAT_initial_rho_vs_alpha.pdf` | Exploratory jointly optimized results | Descriptive optimizer behavior only; not an independent response-memory effect |
| Supp. matched 2-SAT control | `FigS_2SAT_matched_kw_slices.pdf`, `FigS_2SAT_matched_kw_heatmap.pdf` | Matched-$k_w$ response surface | Exact rho=0 control; formula bootstrap; nonzero rho worsens the reported outcome |

All figure assets required by the TeX sources are local to this revision directory. The submission build does not depend on private absolute paths.

Publication-facing labels are regenerated by `make_consistency_figures.py` for Main Fig. 2, the earlier Supplemental figures, MAX-CUT figures, and 2-SAT figures. Main Fig. 5 and the two initialization-robustness figures are copied from the validated three-arm and arbitrary-initial-state analysis outputs; their source scripts are retained as `figure_source_phase5_fixed_transfer.py` and `figure_source_initialization_robustness.py`, and their copied processed inputs are under `source_data/`. Internal method keys identify validated result rows but are not rendered in legends or axes. Main Fig. 1 is generated from `figures/Fig1_update_rules.tex`.
